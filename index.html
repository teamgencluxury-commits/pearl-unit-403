Coastal Luxury Redefined

Welcome to Unit 403 at Pearl Main Beach — an exceptional residence set within one of the Gold Coast's most prestigious beachside addresses. Positioned on Level 4 of this architectural landmark, this 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom apartment offers a sophisticated lifestyle just 53 steps from the sands of Main Beach.

Pearl rises 30 levels above Main Beach Parade, delivering 360-degree views stretching from the Pacific Ocean to the Gold Coast skyline and hinterland beyond. The building's striking curved design and full-height double-glazed glass ensure every apartment is flooded with natural light and connected to the coastal environment.

Developed by Raptis — celebrating over 40 years of architectural excellence on the Gold Coast — Pearl sets a new benchmark for refined coastal living.

Full-height double-glazed glass doors and windows throughout, flooding every room with natural light.
Integrated Miele appliances — fridge, freezer drawer, dishwasher, microwave and gas cooking.
Primary suite complete with private ensuite and balcony access.
Stone benches and full-height Carrara tiling in cutting-edge bathrooms.
2.7m square-set ceilings with luminous LED downlighting throughout.
Secure intercom access and remote garage entry to designated car space.
Pet-friendly building with low body corporate fees and individual water meters.
Ducted air conditioning throughout the apartment.
